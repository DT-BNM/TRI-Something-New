Applied Learning

Capstone Project

Teams apply everything they've learned to a realistic business case — the Cyclistic rider analysis. Cyclistic is a fictional bike-share company seeking to understand how casual riders and annual members use its service differently, so it can develop strategies to convert more casual riders into members.

Central Business Question

"How do casual riders and annual members use Cyclistic differently, and what actions could Cyclistic take to convert more casual riders into annual members?"

Beginner-Friendly

Clear and Intuitive

The business question requires no prior industry knowledge. Teams can begin with simple comparisons before developing deeper findings.

Challenging

Real-World Complexity

The case includes large volumes of imperfect data across multiple files. Teams must clean, consolidate and interpret the data rather than merely report observations.

Comparable

A Fair Playing Field

All teams work from the same base case, enabling fair comparison while allowing different analytical approaches, visual designs and recommendations.

Transferable

Mirrors Real TRI Work

The work mirrors TRI activities such as consolidating data, comparing performance, identifying trends and exceptions, building dashboards and translating findings into decisions.
